My Piano Story - Finding Joy in Every Note

The piano has become an integral part of my life, though I can't pinpoint exactly when or how this love affair began. While I may not possess the innate talent of Arthur Rubinstein, I've discovered a unique gift—excellent sight-reading (Prima vista in Italian) skill, that is, the ability to play pieces exceptionally well at first sight.

This skill, combined with the sheer joy of creating music, has fueled my passion for the instrument. There's something magical about sitting at the keys, knowing that I can bring forth a symphony of sounds with just my fingertips. It's this sense of wonder and accomplishment that keeps me coming back to the piano time and time again.

My love for the piano deepened in an unexpected moment during my ninth-grade year in 1990. As I was playing one day, I was suddenly overwhelmed by a profound feeling, one that words have never been able to reach. In that instant, I understood more clearly than ever why music is beautiful. For the first time, music was no longer something I produced with my fingers; it was a beauty I felt, one that lies beyond the reach of logos, which no words, not even words yet to be invented, could ever describe, nor any image depict. Since that day, the piano has been my way back to that beauty, and an inseparable part of who I am.
